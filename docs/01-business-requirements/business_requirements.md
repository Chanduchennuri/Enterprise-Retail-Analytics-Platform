# Company Overview

RetailX is a multi-channel retail company that sells electronics,
fashion, and home appliances through:

- Website
- Mobile App
- Physical Stores

The company operates:

- 50 Stores
- 5 Warehouses
- 100 Suppliers

Customers can purchase products online or in-store.

Management wants a centralized analytics platform to analyze sales,
inventory, customers, and operational performance.


# Business Goals

1. Increase revenue visibility.
2. Track sales performance across channels.
3. Improve inventory management.
4. Identify high-value customers.
5. Reduce stock shortages.
6. Improve supplier performance tracking.
7. Enable executive reporting.

# Sales Channels

1. E-Commerce Website
2. Mobile Application
3. Physical Stores
4. Third-Party Marketplaces

# Business Processes

## Order-to-Cash

Customer places order
→ Payment processed
→ Inventory allocated
→ Product shipped
→ Revenue recorded

## Procure-to-Pay

Supplier provides inventory
→ Warehouse receives stock
→ Inventory updated
→ Supplier payment issued

## Return-to-Refund

Customer returns product
→ Return validated
→ Inventory adjusted
→ Refund processed

# Current Challenges

1. Sales data exists across multiple systems.
2. Inventory reports are delayed.
3. Revenue reports are inconsistent.
4. Customer analytics are unavailable.
5. Executives lack real-time insights.
6. Data quality issues cause reporting errors.
